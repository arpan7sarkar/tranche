# Sandbox read-only maintenance

Date: 2026-10-12.
Status: Implemented and tested offline; both initial amendments separately approved and applied in the sandbox.

## Existing product readiness

The intended payment pair is a US Personal buyer and a separate US Business seller.
The original US seller referral requested EXPRESS_CHECKOUT, while the merchant-integration response returned PPCP_STANDARD and PPCP_CUSTOM with vetting_status SUBSCRIBED.
The verifier continues to distinguish those requested products according to [PayPal's before-payment onboarding guide](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/seller-onboarding/before-payment/).
Product maintenance adopts an already subscribed PPCP product as the readiness requirement, without replacing the original referral or requesting additional PayPal permissions.
It does not provision a product, enable advanced card processing or claim payment success.
Create a private input file containing {"kind":"onboarding_product","product":"PPCP","mappingVersion":N}, where N is the original mapping version plus one.
The source operation must be waiting or inconclusive with a recorded referral, product_inactive evidence and an unexpired original checkUntil.
The fresh API response must still match the original merchant, tracking ID, platform app, required scopes, payments receivable and confirmed email, and must satisfy the PPCP product check.
The original spec, referral, request ID, request body, attempts, recovery counter and deadline remain unchanged.
The separately approved target context becomes effective for P4 consent checks only after successful verification; the original product and mapping no longer satisfy that check.

## Retire an unused order locally

Create a private input file containing {"kind":"retire_order"} for an order that has not advanced past create_order.
This retires its commitment in this runner; it does not cancel the provider order or revoke permissions at PayPal.
Do not reuse the old checkout link or authorize or capture the abandoned order through another application or dashboard.
Only use this process when this runner controls authorization and capture for the abandoned test; local retirement cannot prevent another credential holder from acting outside it.
The fresh response must identify the same AUTHORIZE order, commitment reference, seller, USD amount and unapproved state, without buyer identity, authorization or capture.
Application atomically blocks the original operation, resolves its local browser wait and releases only its unspent principal, fee bounds and remaining held calls.
Original resource IDs, request, approvals, attempt history, counters and payment result remain recorded.
Future stages and attempts to prepare another operation with that commitment are rejected.
A replacement payment requires a new commitment, new approvals and the same shared allocation.

## Approval and execution

Preparation persists only an audit proposal and sends no request.
Before approval, review the operation, private account association, target product or retirement purpose, and two-call allowance with the contributor.
Approval reserves exactly two additional calls within the persistent 50-call limit, without changing the USD 1 cap or resetting previous counts.
Application performs only OAuth and a known merchant-integration or order GET; it never sends a business POST.

```powershell
pnpm --filter @tranche/agents-service f0:runner maintenance <operation-id> <private-input-file>
pnpm --filter @tranche/agents-service f0:runner approve-maintenance <maintenance-id> <approval-hash>
pnpm --filter @tranche/agents-service f0:runner apply-maintenance <maintenance-id>
pnpm --filter @tranche/agents-service f0:runner inspect <run-id>
```

An outstanding proposal blocks ordinary resume and competing amendments.
Duplicate approval or application cannot reserve or dispatch again.
Unsafe or failed reads preserve the money reservation and conservative remaining call reservation, mark the amendment failed, and require investigation.
A process interruption after claiming also requires investigation; the persisted claim prevents restarting the same amendment from issuing another pair of requests.
No automatic retry, cancellation, arbitrary allowance extension or reservation reset is provided.
The checkout request's missing return and cancel URLs were subsequently addressed for new orders, with tested local informational pages and no automatic payment action.
